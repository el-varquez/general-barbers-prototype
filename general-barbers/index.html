Booking, queue and check-in for a three-branch barbershop

Customers can book and pay a downpayment in the app, while the front desk can add walk-ins in seconds. Both enter one live queue, follow the same chair and payment workflow, and feed the same customer and sales records.

The flow at a glance

online booking or walk-in — one queue, five visit statuses
BookedThe customer pays the 50% downpayment in the app. A QR code is generated.
In queueFront desk scans the QR on arrival. Position and wait show on the phone.
In the chairThe barber scans the same QR at the chair and taps Start cut.
Cut doneThe barber taps Mark as done. The next customer is notified automatically.
CompletedFront desk scans again, collects the balance, and the rating prompt goes out.
