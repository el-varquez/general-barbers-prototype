01 · Where the day leaks

Four things that go wrong every week

No-shows

A Messenger booking costs nothing to break. The chair sits empty, the barber waits, the walk-in already left.

“Who’s next?”

Barbers ask the counter, customers hover by the chair, and the receptionist is the only one who knows the order.

Cash on paper

Payments go on a list. Nobody can say what each barber earned this week without adding it up by hand.

No customer list

Hundreds of regulars, and not one phone number the shop can reach when a slot opens up.

04 · What the shop gets

What to decide before a pilot

  • The downpayment: 50%, a flat ₱100, or none for regulars.
  • Who calls the next customer: the barber's Mark as done, or the front desk's completion scan.
  • Walk-ins and add-ons: entered at the counter so the report stays true without a POS.
  • Which sign-in each barber uses, so their staff account matches on the first try.
  • Pilot at JP Laurel with four chairs, then Polomolok and Calumpang.
